Focus Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Focus Pulse ("the app") helps you block distracting apps and focus. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: everything stays on your phone. Focus Pulse has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads.

Information the app uses

Accessibility Service

To block apps, Focus Pulse uses Android's Accessibility Service API. The service learns which app is currently open (its package name, such as com.example.app). It uses that only to close apps you chose to block during a focus session, a sprint or on your always-blocked list, and to show a "blocked" screen.

The service does not read screen content, text, messages, passwords or anything you type. It doesn't record your activity, and it doesn't send this information anywhere. It only works after you agree to an in-app explanation and turn it on in Android's Settings. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

Usage access

If you allow it, Focus Pulse reads Android's app usage statistics to show your daily screen time on the Home and Statistics screens. This data is read on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can withdraw this permission at any time in Settings > Usage access.

Installed apps

Focus Pulse lists the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon, so you can choose which ones to block or allow. This list stays on your phone.

Data you create

Your focus sessions, sprints, blocked and allowed app lists, block history, affirmations, life goals and any audio files you add to the Library are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They aren't backed up to the cloud by Focus Pulse, and they're deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Focus Pulse Pro (passes and lifetime) is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Focus Pulse never sees your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of which Pro purchases your Google account owns, and stores that status on your phone so Pro keeps working offline.

Sharing

Focus Pulse does not sell, share or transfer any information to anyone. The only network connection the app makes is to Google Play, to show prices and handle purchases.

Children

Focus Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Turn off the Accessibility Service or usage access at any time in Android Settings.
  • Delete all app data with Settings > Apps > Focus Pulse > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
